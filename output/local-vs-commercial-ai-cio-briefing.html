Recommended architecture

Local-first where it earns the right.

Route local

Private · repetitive · bounded

Classification, extraction with validated schemas, offline workflows, and interactive tasks where TTFT matters.

Policy
router
Route commercial

Complex · concise · elastic

High-value reasoning, executive content, variable workloads, and requests where completion reliability dominates.

Escalate automatically when the local response hits a token ceiling, fails schema validation, or falls below a quality threshold.
